Why Ronin402

Built for agents, secured for humans

Four primitives that let an AI agent operate a real-world-asset strategy end-to-end, while your keys — and your limits — stay yours.

Pay-per-call via x402

Agents pay for price quotes, market data and execution requests on the fly using the x402 payment header — no subscriptions, no API keys, no idle accounts.

On-chain limits & escrow

Smart contracts hold funds in escrow and enforce spending caps, slippage bounds and per-agent allowances before a single settlement leg fires.

Approval-free RWA trading

Once a policy is signed, agents execute within it autonomously — rebalancing, hedging and settling tokenized real-world assets without a wallet pop-up for every trade.

ETH-native, degen UX

No permissioned onboarding, no KYC gate at the protocol layer. Connect a wallet, fund a vault, set limits, and let the agent rip.

Flow

From intent to settlement, in five hops

Every trade an agent makes moves through the same metered, on-chain-verified pipeline.

  1. 01

    Set the policy

    You define spending limits, allowed assets, and slippage/risk bounds in a smart contract vault. You sign once.

  2. 02

    Agent requests a quote

    Your agent calls a data or pricing endpoint. The server responds 402 Payment Required with an x402 payment challenge.

  3. 03

    Agent pays & receives data

    The agent settles the micro-payment from its operating balance and receives the quote, dataset or execution route.

  4. 04

    Contract checks the trade

    Before execution, the vault contract verifies the trade against your limits, escrow balance and asset allowlist.

  5. 05

    Settlement, on-chain

    Funds move atomically between escrow and the RWA venue. The result — and the receipt — are logged on-chain.

Security posture

Autonomy, bounded by design

The agent can act fast because the contract can't be talked out of the rules.

Hard spending caps

Per-agent, per-asset and per-period limits are enforced in the vault contract, not in agent code.

Non-custodial by default

You hold the vault's admin key. Agents get scoped session permissions, never withdrawal rights.

Revocable, always

Pause or revoke an agent's policy in one transaction — no waiting on off-chain infrastructure.

Slippage & route checks

Execution routes returned via x402 are validated on-chain before funds move.

On-chain receipts

Every payment and settlement leaves an auditable, on-chain trail tied to the originating policy.

Independent audits

Vault and escrow contracts are reviewed before mainnet limits are raised beyond testnet caps.
